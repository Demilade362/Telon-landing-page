Frequently Asked Questions.

Is Kasai Custodial?

No. Kasai is completely non-custodial. Your private keys never leave your mobile vault, and agents trade through a secure proxy. We cannot withdraw your funds.

How does Kasai make money?

We align our success with yours by taking a 10% performance fee on the net profits generated by your AI Agent. If you don't profit, we don't profit.

Which chains are supported?

We currently support Solana and all major EVM chains (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon) depending on your agent tier.

Can the agent be rug-pulled?

Our AI features a built-in Rug Shield. It simulates every transaction on a shadow fork and continuously monitors smart contracts for malicious changes or liquidity withdrawals.
