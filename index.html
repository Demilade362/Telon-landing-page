Onboarding

How to get started.

Follow these 3 steps to activate your autonomous wealth engine.

01

Deploy your Mobile Vault

Download the Telon Mobile App and create your non-custodial wallet. This is your "Source of Truth". Your recovery phrase is encrypted in your device's Secure Enclave.

02

Link the Telon Nexus

Install the Chrome Extension and scan the "Nexus QR" with your mobile app. Now, whenever you interact with a DeFi site on your desktop, the AI Shield will audit the contract and prompt your phone for a biometric signature.

03

Activate your AI Agent

Open the "Agent Dashboard" in your app. Move a small amount of SOL or ETH from your main vault into the Agent Desk. Set your risk profile (Conservative to DeGen) and let the AI start finding alpha.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions.

Is Telon Custodial?

No. Telon is completely non-custodial. Your private keys never leave your mobile vault, and agents trade through a secure proxy. We cannot withdraw your funds.

How does Telon make money?

We charge a small 0.3% protocol fee on swaps and bridge transactions. We also take a 10% performance fee on the net profits generated by your AI Agent.

Which chains are supported?

We currently support Solana and all major EVM chains (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon) depending on your agent tier.

Can the agent be rug-pulled?

Our AI features a built-in Rug Shield. It simulates every transaction on a shadow fork and continuously monitors smart contracts for malicious changes or liquidity withdrawals.
