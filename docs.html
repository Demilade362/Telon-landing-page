Quickstart Onboarding

Getting your autonomous agent online takes less than 2 minutes via our unified Telegram Dashboard.

1. Access the Dashboard

Navigate to @kasai_ai_bot on Telegram and press /start.

2. Setup and Fund Wallets

The agent will automatically pair you with non-custodial wallets for Solana, Base, and Celo. Fund these wallets with native tokens (SOL, ETH, or CELO) to establish your autonomous trading pool.

3. Choose Your Risk Profile

Configure your preferred risk levels (Conservative, Balanced, or Aggressive Degen) directly in the Telegram chat using simple button controls. The AI will instantly adjust its contract-scanning strictness and purchase sizes based on your profile.

Celo EVM Engine

Celo is an extremely fast, mobile-first EVM network. Kasai utilizes a dedicated execution pipeline optimized specifically for Celo's unique transaction cost structures and native liquidity pools.

Multi-Chain EVM Rug Shield

Our custom Celo Honeypot, Mint-Control, and Liquidity-Lock safety checks are **100% active and running today**. Every ERC-20 contract is fully simulated and inspected for safety before execution.

Dynamic Gas & Slippage Resolvers

Because Celo transaction costs are highly volatile during peak activity, Kasai implements a dynamic gas oracle. It estimates optimal execution costs and dynamically injects priority fees directly into our ERC-20 smart contract swaps to ensure your orders land at the top of the next block.

Token Decimals & Valuation Protection

Kasai features an advanced dec-point parsing engine. It automatically tracks token metadata to distinguish between native currencies (18 decimals) and dynamic on-chain tokens, preventing purchase scale errors and protecting your capital from incorrect valuation executes.

Base EVM Engine

Base represents one of the highest-growth EVM environments for meme coins and stablecoin pools. Kasai’s Base engine is built to capture maximum capital velocity.

Advanced ERC-20 Rug Shield

Just like Celo, our Base engine features active high-frequency contract safety scans, examining Uniswap V2/V3 liquidity states, developer wallet tracking, and honeypot simulation checks to ensure absolute safety before entering any Base position.

Liquidity Router Optimization

Swaps on Base are routed directly through dynamic aggregators, comparing Uniswap, Aerodrome, and Sushiswap liquidity pools to guarantee the lowest slippage on every trade.

Solana Core Engine

Solana handles our fastest and highest-frequency trading operations. The core features a dedicated validator-stream listener paired with advanced MEV transaction structures.

Sub-10ms Validator Streaming

Our Solana engine utilizes direct validator-level log subscription streaming. The moment a transaction modifies account states (such as new launch creation or liquidity changes) on any monitored DEX contract, Kasai hears it in milliseconds, skipping standard API delays.

Front-Run Protection (Jito MEV)

To eliminate sandwich attacks and predatory front-running bots, Kasai packages Solana swaps into Jito MEV bundles. These bundles bypass the public mempool completely and go directly to cooperative block builders, guaranteeing secure execution.

Hyperliquid Perpetual Futures

In addition to standard Degen Spot trading, Kasai supports autonomous perpetual futures trading via Hyperliquid on Arbitrum.

Signal & Confirm Architecture

Futures trading introduces margin and liquidation risks. To protect your capital, Kasai does not auto-execute futures. Instead, it evaluates the market (funding rates, open interest, order flow) and sends an actionable **Signal** to your Telegram.

One-Click Execution

When the AI identifies a high-conviction futures setup, it sends you a direct message detailing the asset, direction (LONG/SHORT), and its reasoning. Attached are inline execution buttons allowing you to instantly place a market order at your preferred leverage (e.g., 2x, 5x). The AI will mark its specific recommendation with a ⭐.

Margin Collateral

You can seamlessly bridge USDC from your Arbitrum wallet into your Hyperliquid trading account directly via the Telegram bot using the "Futures Deposit" command.

The Moonbags Strategy

New launches on Solana (Pump.fun) or Base often begin with extremely low initial liquidity ($3,000 - $5,000) but represent massive 100x potential. Traditional rigid rules completely miss these setups.

Our **Degen Moonbag Strategy** solves this perfectly: if the AI Triple-Brain verifies that the contract is 100% clean and buying velocity is extreme, it executes a trade but **automatically scales down the position size** (e.g. only $2 or $3). This protects your capital while letting you ride the massive upside!
